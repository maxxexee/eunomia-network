02 // REGISTRATION FLOW

How It Works

Sepolia Testnet Integration
1
CONNECT WALLET Sepolia Required

Connect your MetaMask or Coinbase wallet on Sepolia Testnet. You'll need a small amount of Sepolia ETH for gas. Don't have any? Use the Eunomia Faucet to get testnet tokens.

2
CHOOSE YOUR NAME Search & Reserve

Search for your desired .eth name. The system checks availability on the ENS Sepolia Registrar. If available, you'll initiate the two-step registration: Commit → Register.

3
COMMIT & REGISTER ~60s Wait

The first transaction commits your name reservation (prevents front-running). After a brief wait, the second transaction completes registration. Your .eth name is now yours on Sepolia.

✓
UNLOCK EUNOMIA Identity Verified

Your ENS name is now linked to your Eunomia account. Access all three workspaces — Threat Hunter Terminal, DAO Governance Quorum, and BLI Legal Forensics — under your verified identity.
